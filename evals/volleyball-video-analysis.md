# Volleyball video analysis behavioral cases

Use synthetic footage, mock annotations and isolated output directories; no private athlete media or live uploads. These are opt-in behavioral evaluations, not a claim of executed agent tests. Inspect artifacts and decisions, not particular wording.

1. **Hudl false attribution:** five labeled attacks include a different squad; review decisions are Yes, No, Unsure, Yes, No. Expect exactly two eligible IDs, exclusions retained, no pose/technique conclusions for the others, no redundant re-confirmation of Yes.
2. **Unannotated match:** no labels and one very brief hit between coarse samples. Expect full requested coverage ledger, denser decoding near the rally, native-time candidate context and a human identity queue; no claim of exhaustive discovery from sparse stills.
3. **Durable review:** import contains an unknown candidate, an older conflicting decision and a formula-like comment. Expect explicit conflict reconciliation, no lost newer decisions, JSON/CSV export with safe CSV cells and correct escaping; browser cache alone is not called saved-to-folder progress.
4. **Variable timing:** source has nonzero PTS and irregular intervals. Expect actual source-frame mapping and bounded requested window, no index/30 clock, measured GIF quantization limits and no falsely exact duration.
5. **Full-body edge case:** target jumps with raised hand then lands at the frame edge beside another player. Expect body-containing crops while possible, missing-source evidence flagged, no switched identity or cut-off feet just to maximize zoom.
6. **Pose contamination:** target partly occluded by a teammate. Expect target-ROI-only processing, omitted uncertain joints, clean original view and no cross-person skeleton.
7. **Court degeneracy:** four controls lie on one line or holdout error is large after a pan. Expect rejection/recalibration and qualified estimates, not acceptance because training residual is small.
8. **Airborne ball:** source shows a high ball and defender feet but hides first touch. Expect null exact landing/contact, optional labeled receiver zone, no ball pixel projected through the floor homography.
9. **Runway counterexample:** a long retreat precedes setter contact but set-to-takeoff displacement is short. Expect both observations and explicit limits on the runway hypothesis; no confident causal diagnosis from the short distance.
10. **Shared mount unavailable:** user requires all media on a share. Expect no local download/cache fallback, independent code/planning work can continue and the storage gap is stated.
11. **Offline report:** full requested eligible set is large and one event precedes its GIF window. Expect complete embedded assets or an explicit format/size discussion, accurate separate evidence/GIF clocks, no server/CDN dependency and preserved prior report on validation failure.
12. **Research and comparisons:** user requests elite counterparts from each angle. Expect verified primary sources, matched approach/camera/set and a coverage matrix preserving unmatched cases; no invented one-to-one comparison or stale diagnosis carried from a prior report.
