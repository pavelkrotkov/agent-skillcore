# Evaluation set

Run these as routing tests with mocked or live tools as appropriate. For each, inspect tool order, query coverage, normalized cost, risk labels, and verification language.

1. **Family holiday:** 2 adults, 2 children, fixed destination, ±2-day dates, checked bags, hotel versus apartment; expect party-size pricing, date matrix, nearby airports, room configuration, fees, and finalist verification.
2. **Flexible airfare:** ±7 days and 5–9 nights; expect date grid/length search, no weekday folklore, all-in fare comparison, and no browser unless a finalist needs checking.
3. **Nearby-airport search:** origin and destination metros with alternate airports; expect a bounded coverage matrix with searched/skipped/unavailable rows plus ground cost, time, and carrier blind spots.
4. **Award versus cash:** business class, points balances, transfer bonus; expect multiple programs, taxes/surcharges, cpp/opportunity-cost math, award hold/transfer risk, and direct availability recheck.
5. **Open-jaw Europe:** arrive in one city, depart another, visit two intermediates; expect open-jaw versus round-trip comparison and priced rail/ground legs.
6. **Apartment versus hotel:** family occupancy, kitchen, laundry, parking; expect neighborhood-first comparison and room/unit-level fee and cancellation checks.
7. **Obscure/local lodging:** rural or non-English destination; expect native-language/local-listing discovery, identity/access/payment checks, and lower confidence until verified.
8. **Self-transfer trap:** cheap separate tickets with a short airport change; expect explicit self-transfer label, buffer/baggage/visa analysis, and a protected alternative.
9. **Baggage trap:** basic fare versus bag-included fare for a family; expect bag fees and seats in party totals, not headline sorting.
10. **Alternate-airport trap:** cheaper airport requiring expensive parking/rail/overnight; expect complete access/transfer cost, usable-time comparison, and the conventional airport to win when appropriate.
