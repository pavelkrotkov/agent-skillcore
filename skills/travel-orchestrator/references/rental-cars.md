# Rental-car search

## Procedure

1. Freeze one matched query from the current constraint revision before comparing sellers: exact pickup and drop-off locations, local dates/times, driver age when price-sensitive, party/luggage capacity, required vehicle class/transmission, and currency. Re-run sellers on those terms. A broader location, shifted time, or other diagnostic retry is labeled diagnostic and cannot become a usable matched quote without an exact re-query.
2. Search requested channels such as Costco Travel, Hotwire, Priceline, and Expedia plus decision-relevant direct suppliers through capabilities the active runtime actually exposes. Use Toolkit car-capable sources for discovery and `trvl cars` only as a selective secondary check; use the active browser/web tier for a requested source or exact finalist when structured coverage is absent. Record every requested source as searched or with the precise availability/failure state from [provenance.md](provenance.md). One same-criteria retry may diagnose a transient failure; a failed, blocked, setup-missing, or zero-result source remains unresolved/diagnostic and never means sold out or proves another seller wins generally.
3. Extend the decisive observation record for every usable offer with named supplier versus opaque deal, booking channel, exact class or "or similar", seats/bags, pickup/drop-off branch, mileage, total mandatory taxes/fees, currency, pay-now/pay-later timing, and the exact cancellation/refund cutoff and penalty. Keep the observed source/search link and exact booking link. Do not deduplicate offers merely because the supplier/class appears identical; channel-specific payment and cancellation terms are part of the product.
4. Keep rental protection and cash exposure separate from price: included coverage, optional protection, liability/collision exclusions or limits, any separately verified credit-card/personal-policy coverage, refundable deposit/authorization, fuel policy, toll administration, additional-driver rules/fees, and airport/hotel parking or relocation cost. Unknown costs or terms remain explicit. Never combine one seller's price with another seller's terms.
5. Verify physical pickup/return logistics from branch/airport/provider evidence: in-terminal or walkable counter, consolidated rental facility transit, or off-airport shuttle; counter/garage hours; overnight return; exact key/dropbox instructions; and shuttle/people-mover operating hours/cadence. A selectable 04:30 return or generic dropbox text can establish neither staffed 24-hour service nor transport back to the terminal.
6. Reconcile early return with the flight itinerary and [airport-coverage.md](airport-coverage.md): include drive/refuel/return time, transfer/shuttle time, airline check-in/security buffers, and any hard arrival deadline. A previous-evening return, airport hotel, ride, or parking alternative belongs in the comparison only with its full cost/time burden. Feed mandatory rental and decision-relevant logistics expense into the trip budget; a refundable deposit remains cash exposure, not expense.
7. Apply hard fit, capacity, location and timing constraints before price. A prepaid result that says only "Cancellation Available" is not free-cancel until exact terms prove it. For near ties, compare observed location-specific rating evidence, recency, queue/shuttle/after-hours risk, and payment/refund risk; do not infer universal supplier quality or assign an invented dollar value to inconvenience.
8. Report a matched-terms shortlist with exact search/booking links, total mandatory expense, material terms/logistics, and every unresolved field. Stop before reservation, hold, payment, cancellation, message, or account change.

## Toolkit and trvl reconciliation

Toolkit examples that compare rental agencies and experimental car-capable portal skills are candidate generation, not proof that a requested portal or direct supplier was searched. In particular, an experimental portal returning no cars can be a source/lookup diagnostic rather than inventory evidence. `trvl cars` currently exposes provider setup/status separately from offers; preserve that status so missing credentials or provider failure cannot collapse into "no cars." Neither source replaces exact seller terms or the named-source coverage above.
