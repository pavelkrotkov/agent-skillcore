# Local and obscure discovery

## Procedure

1. Define the locality before the listing: neighborhood, transit stop, airport/rail access, local spelling, and acceptable radius. Search the destination in its native language and script plus local terms for hotel, guesthouse, apartment, pension, ryokan, agriturismo, riad, or the relevant lodging type.
2. Use Toolkit discovery skills (`atlas-obscura`, `wikipedia-airports`, `sutochno`, `vrbo`, `tripadvisor`, and `compare-hotels` where relevant) and `trvl` destination/accommodation tools. Treat map pins, social posts, and directories as leads; validate identity, address, dates, capacity, reviews, and payment/cancellation terms elsewhere.
3. Search local booking engines and the property’s direct site when mainstream inventory is thin. Keep language and currency explicit. Translate only after preserving the original property name, policy text, and price components.
4. For rural or obscure lodging, verify access hours, check-in method, road/rail transfer, heating/air-conditioning, bathroom arrangement, internet, bedding, deposits, tourist taxes, and whether the listing is an entire unit or a room in a shared home.
5. Do not recommend a local listing solely because it is cheaper or has a high score. Give lower confidence when identity, availability, terms, or payment protections remain unverified.
